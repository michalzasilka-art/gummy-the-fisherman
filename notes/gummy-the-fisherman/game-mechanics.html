Gummy - Game Mechanics

Back to Gummy the Fisherman · Player-facing rules: How to Play

These numbers come from the Production game code (gummy-fisherman-copy/index.html).

Turns

  • Every player answers 2 sentences per round, so a round has players × 2 turns.
  • Running out of time counts as a wrong answer, with speed 0.
  • The fish swims closer to the surface as the team gets more answers right.

Fish weight (kilos)

Every answer adds kilos to the fish:

right answer = (0.3 + 0.7 × speed) kg × streak bonus
wrong answer = 0 kg
speed        = share of the answer time still left (1 = instant, 0 = last moment)
streak bonus = the player's OWN right answers in a row, all game: 1st ×1, 2nd ×1.25, 3rd ×1.5 … up to ×3.5
  • Team / 6p: one fish per round, which weighs the sum of every player's kilos that round.
  • Solo: each player's fish weighs only their own kilos.
  • Every fish weighs at least 0.1 kg.
  • Junk (0.10 kg, 0 points, one of the place's junk items) comes from the catch bar: Solo = that player missed the net, Team / 6p = nobody got it into the net.

Catch bar

After every round's answers, each player gets one try, one after the other. A fish swims from side to side along a bar; the player taps the CATCH! button on their own player card (or Space) while it is inside the net.

net width   = 9 % of the bar + 25 % × (round score / 10)      → 0/10: 9 %, 5/10: 21.5 %, 10/10: 34 %
round score = each answer worth 5: 3 for right + up to 2 for speed
crossing    = 1.35 s (round 1), 1.2 s (round 2), 1.05 s (round 3), 0.95 s (bonus round) end to end
time        = 10 s to tap, then it counts as a miss
  • The net sits somewhere new on every try.
  • Solo: in the net = the fish your answers made; missed = junk.
  • Team / 6p: the team's fish weighs × (0.5 + hits / players): half the team = ×1, everyone = ×1.5. Nobody in = junk.

Which fish bites

  • Each catch is picked at random from the fish not yet in the Fish Index, all with the same chance.
  • Once the whole index has been caught, any fish can bite again.
  • Production has no rarities: every fish is just a "Fish". (Test and Phone still use rarities; see Versions.)

Fish price ($)

price = 100 × (1 + 0.5 × size) × round multiplier × (0.75 + 0.5 × accuracy)
size             = where the weight falls in that fish's own range (lightest 0 → heaviest 1)
round multiplier = 1 / 1.5 / 2 for Sunny Pond / Sunset River / Midnight Ocean
accuracy         = share of right answers behind the catch

The money adds up in the lobby's $ total. (In Test and Phone, the base price depends on rarity: Common $25, Rare $100, Epic $300, Legendary $1,500, Mythical $10,000.)

Points

fish points  = 100 × kilos (junk 0)        Team / 6p: shared equally by the players in the net (a miss gets 0)
speed points = up to 50 per RIGHT answer    (instant 50 … at the buzzer 0)

The catch screen shows each player's points for the round; the weigh-in shows the kilos and the points; the places are ranked by points.

Winning

After round 3 (or the bonus round) the fish go on the scale. The most points win (kilos break a tie).

Saved in the browser

The Fish Index (catch counts and best kilos per fish, also per character), stats, lobby settings and the chosen package are saved in localStorage. The game also sends Fish Index stats to /api/gummy; see Ideas and To-do.
