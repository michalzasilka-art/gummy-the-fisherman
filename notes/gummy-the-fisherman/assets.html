Where the files are

The original art, videos and character pictures are kept in the team's project files. The game's own copies are in the Production game folder (gummy-fisherman-copy).

In the Production game

art/: - Round backgrounds: lake-level1-* (one per rod), lake-level2, lake-level3 - Solo rounds: solo-round1–3, solo-boat, solo-dock - 6p rounds: six-round1–3, six-final (final photo), six-bar-bg, six-crown.svg - Gummy holding the catch: gummy-catch, gummy-catch2, gummy-catch3 - Cards and heroes: card-*, hero-* - Other: scale, board, title, loading-bg, lobby-lake, chain, badges/, and the rods rod-*

fish/: the 16 fish and old-boot (painted .webp files); see Fish Index.

video/: - Lobby backgrounds: lobby-bg, lobby-bg2, lobby-bg3, lobby-bg4 (slowed down and looped) - Round 1 moving background: round1-bg - Character loops: pfp-stella, pfp-foxino, pfp-pix, pfp-ghost, pfp-rob, pfp-gummy - 6p pull cutscenes: six-pull (day), six-pull2 (sunset), six-pull3 (night, from THENEW.mp4)

Raw material

  • Character pictures (*PPF.png) and their video versions (*PFPvideo.mp4)
  • Round backgrounds (*PTbackround.png, Round1Background.webp, SoloBackround.png, BoatBackround.png)
  • The title, menu and loading backgrounds, and the Gummy Index background
  • Fishes/: raw fish art
  • content/: the package builder and sample content
  • references/: UI references
  • Several Higgsfield and Gemini clips
