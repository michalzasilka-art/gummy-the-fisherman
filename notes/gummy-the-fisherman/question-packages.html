Gummy - Question Packages

Back to Gummy the Fisherman · Rules: How to Play

Before every game the players pick one package in the lobby. Each package is one grammar topic from Foxino content (CEFR level A0–A2). The game mode is Right word into the sentence: the sentence has a gap, and the player picks the word that fits from the right answer plus up to 2 wrong ones. A hint in brackets, like (sleep), shows the base word.

The questions are split over the 3 rounds, but every game mixes all of a package's questions over the rounds again. The live game has 962 questions in 5 packages.

Level Package Questions
A0 Articles (a – an) 160
A1 Comparative adjectives (-er forms – irregular forms) 172
A1 Present simple (questions – short answers) 310
A2 Past continuous (positive) 160
A2 Connecting words (because – so) 160

How packages are made: Foxino gap-fill content (JSON) → content/build-packages.js → gummy-fisherman-copy/packages.js (see Tech and Deployment).

In the lists below, the right answer is bold and the wrong choices are ~~struck through~~.
