Gummy - Modes and Settings

Back to Gummy the Fisherman

Player modes (Production)

Mode What it is
Team Up to 4 players. One fish per round for the whole team.
Solo Up to 4 players. Every player catches their own fish every round, from their own answers. Solo rounds have their own backgrounds (boat and dock pictures).
6p Team rules with 6 players: Rob and Gummy join in as players 5 and 6. A pull cutscene plays after every round (day, sunset, night), and the game ends on a final group photo with a crown and a bottom bar.

Question mode

Production has one mode: Right word into the sentence. The sentence has a gap, and the player picks the word that fits, e.g. She ___ a big dog → has / have / having. Older versions also had Wrong word in a sentence and Choose the sentence.

Settings (button in the top left of the lobby)

  • Players: Team / Solo / 6p
  • More → Phone mode: players join on their phones with a QR code or a 6-digit code, then answer their turns on the phone (like Kahoot). Up to 8 phones. The big screen is the host, and the phones open gummy-fisherman-mobile/play.html. It runs through PeerJS.
  • More → Answer time per player: gives every player their own answer time, with a slider each.
  • More → Developer mode: shows "Finish with false / correct answers" buttons in every round, for testing. Off by default.

Lobby controls

  • Answer time slider (default 12 s)
  • Package picker: see Question Packages
  • How to play and Fish index icon buttons on each side of Start fishing
