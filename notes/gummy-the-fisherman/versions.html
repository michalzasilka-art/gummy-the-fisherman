Production is the main game. "Gummy the Fisherman" means Production unless said otherwise. The game is played on 16:9 and 4:3 screens (projectors and boards), so phone layouts aren't needed except in the Phone version.

The built-in sentence set (Test and Phone) has 3 levels × 50 sentences. Each sentence is tagged with one of 39 grammar rules (with English and Czech explanations), for example 3rd person -s, be, plurals, articles, past simple, conditionals and used to.
